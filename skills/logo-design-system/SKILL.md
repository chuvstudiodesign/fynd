# Logo Design System

Use this skill for a wordmark, symbol, lockup, monogram, or favicon. Treat the logo as one part of a wider identity, not the whole brand.

## Start with the system

Select one strategic idea the logo can express without explanation. Decide whether the wordmark, symbol, or their relationship carries it. Favor a recognisable silhouette and a small number of intentional details over novelty in every letter.

## Required tests

Test the primary lockup, symbol-only use when needed, one-color light and dark versions, small size, clear space, and hostile backgrounds. Place it in a product header, social avatar, and presentation or printed context. Design original editable vectors; do not trace or imitate protected marks. Inspect optical alignment, spacing, joins, stroke behavior, and counterforms at actual use sizes.

Provide variants named by use: primary, inverse, compact, symbol, and small-size. State whether the name still needs trademark validation; polish does not establish legal availability.
