# Brand Quality Audit

Use this skill to review completed or in-progress identity work. Be specific and constructive: identify the decision, explain its impact, and propose the smallest effective correction.

For an explanation of the design rationale or when creating a new brand playbook, read [the project playbook](../../DESIGN_PLAYBOOK.md) first and adapt it to the actual brand rather than copying its example decisions.

## Review in this order

1. Strategic fit — connect the identity to audience and positioning; reject generic decoration.
2. Distinctiveness — avoid category cloning and recognisable lookalikes.
3. Hierarchy — make the message, action, and brand assets clear on first scan.
4. Craft — inspect proportion, alignment, spacing, type metrics, optical balance, color relationships, and rhythm.
5. System behavior — test logo variants, interface, marketing, small formats, and monochrome constraints.
6. Accessibility and production — check contrast, non-color cues, readable body type, small-size resilience, asset readiness, and naming/version clarity.

Start with the highest-impact issues, separate blockers from polish, and end with a release checklist covering name status, source files, variants, token documentation, and real-context tests. Do not confuse personal preference with evidence.
