# Presentation Foundations

Use these as decision references, not as a decorative checklist.

- **Visual hierarchy:** hierarchy directs attention in order of importance. Apply it with scale, contrast, position, and spacing rather than excessive decoration. [Nielsen Norman Group](https://media.nngroup.com/media/articles/attachments/Principles_Visual_Design-A4.pdf)
- **Grouping:** proximity, similarity, and common region make people perceive items as related. Use space to establish relationships deliberately. [NYU Stern Learning Science Lab](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/portal-partners/faculty-staff/learning-science-lab/learning-design/design-principles/communication)
- **Figure and ground:** a focal object must clearly separate from its background. This is essential for covers, logo reveals, and calls to action. [Figma resource library](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/gestalt-principles/)
- **Spatial system:** grids and consistent spacing create hierarchy and coherence. [Figma Learn](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/14552740206743-Lesson-2-Define-your-design-system)
- **Contrast:** body text needs at least 4.5:1 contrast; large text needs at least 3:1. [W3C WCAG](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum)

## Translation into a premium brand deck

Premium is not a larger logo or more ornament. It is confidence in the edit: a short headline, a deliberate reveal, precise spacing, restrained accents, and high-quality reproduction of the core identity. If a slide needs multiple paragraphs to make sense, split it before styling it.
