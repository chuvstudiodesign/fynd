# Brand Visual Direction

Use this skill to turn an approved strategy into a few distinct, ownable creative directions. Each route is a coherent system, not a moodboard of attractive references.

## Design each route from a product truth

For every route, define the central metaphor, emotional register, color behavior, typography character, image or illustration treatment, graphic motif, and motion cues when relevant. Test how the idea appears in a wordmark, interface, social post, deck, and small-format icon. Identify the risk of category sameness or short-lived fashion.

Use contrast deliberately: one dominant visual voice, a supporting system, and a controlled accent. Generous whitespace, clear scale, and purposeful rhythm normally create more authority than extra decoration.

## Avoid

- literal illustrations of generic category terms when an abstract product truth is more ownable;
- copying a competitor’s recognisable composition, color signature, or logo gesture;
- relying on one large hero treatment that fails in small or monochrome contexts.

Present 2–3 genuinely different routes, recommend one using strategy and scalability criteria, and include an art-direction prompt plus application tests.
